Gina Chiriac’s French pocket course teaches you to draft, cut and sew a professional slant pocket for trousers and skirts. It explains how to achieve a stable opening without rippling, strain or distortion, from the pattern through final construction.

Practical programme

From pattern to a professional pocket

A French pocket, also known as a slant or diagonal pocket, must combine elegance, comfort and stability. The course explains not only how to sew it, but why placement, angle, depth and reinforcement prevent rippled openings and distorted pocket bags.

What you will learn

  • Draft the pocket from a trouser or skirt block
  • Position the opening for high- or low-rise garments
  • Choose the correct angle and depth
  • Size the pocket bag to the hand
  • Reinforce the bias-cut opening against distortion
  • Sew a stable, durable and accurately aligned pocket

Course structure

01 · Pocket anatomy and pattern drafting
02 · Position, angle and depth
03 · Cutting and bias reinforcement
04 · Professional step-by-step assembly
05 · Bar tacks, finishing and quality control
Professional method

A modern, repeatable technique

The workflow uses the iron, sewing machine and precise tension control without hand basting. Every step explains the technical purpose of the operation so that the result can be repeated consistently.

Who this course is for

Beginners, sewing enthusiasts, fashion and pattern-making students, tailors and dressmakers who want to master an essential detail for trousers and skirts.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are French pockets and slant pockets the same?

The names commonly describe the same pocket type with an angled opening, often used on trousers and skirts.

Is the course suitable for beginners?

Yes. It is a beginner-level course and each stage is explained progressively.

Will I learn to draft the pocket pattern?

Yes. The course starts from the garment block and covers the opening and pocket-bag pattern.

Do I need to hand baste?

No. The method uses the iron, sewing machine and accurate tension control.
