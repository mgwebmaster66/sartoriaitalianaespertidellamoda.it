Gina Chiriac’s straight skirt course teaches you to draft a pattern from real body measurements, cut fabric and sew a complete skirt with darts, a back vent, invisible zipper, waistband and professional finishes. It is suitable for complete beginners.

Practical programme

From body measurements to a professional skirt

The straight skirt block is a foundation of bespoke pattern making and can be developed into pencil, wrap and shaped skirts. Instead of tracing a ready-made pattern, you learn how to build one for a real body and correct it for balanced proportions and fit.

What you will learn

  • Take accurate body measurements with the Chiriac Method
  • Draft a custom straight-skirt pattern from scratch
  • Understand grain line, warp, weft and bias
  • Position the pattern and cut fabric precisely
  • Sew darts, a back vent and an invisible zipper
  • Make a waistband, buttonhole and blind hem
  • Correct the fit and create a reusable pattern

Course structure

01 · Foundations: tools, fabric and measurements
02 · Made-to-measure pattern drafting
03 · Professional pattern layout and cutting
04 · Construction: darts, vent, zipper, waistband and buttonhole
05 · Haute couture finishing
06 · Fit corrections and reusable pattern
Professional method

What makes the Chiriac Method different

The Chiriac Method begins with real body measurements to create balanced proportions and a made-to-measure fit. The course combines pattern drafting, cutting, garment construction and haute couture finishing in a clear, repeatable workflow.

Who this course is for

Complete beginners, fashion and pattern-making students, sewing enthusiasts and experienced makers seeking more accurate, professional results. Techniques are shown for domestic and industrial sewing machines.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is this course suitable for complete beginners?

Yes. It starts with tools, fabric structure and measurements, then progresses step by step.

Is a ready-made pattern supplied?

The core skill is drafting the pattern from your own measurements and correcting it into a reusable block.

What will I make?

A complete straight skirt with darts, back vent, invisible zipper, waistband, buttonhole and blind hem.

Can I use a domestic sewing machine?

Yes. The techniques are explained for both domestic and industrial sewing machines.
