THE CHIRIAC METHOD

Learn sewing from pattern to professional finish

Professional garment making is more than joining pieces of fabric. A reliable result begins with measurements and pattern balance, continues with accurate grain placement and cutting, and depends on controlled construction and finishing.

  • Made-to-measure pattern drafting from real body measurements
  • Pattern making for skirts, trousers and garment details
  • Accurate fabric layout, grain direction and cutting
  • Professional sewing techniques for clean, durable construction
  • Fit correction and reusable pattern development
  • Haute couture-inspired finishing techniques

Who are these courses for?

Complete beginners, self-taught sewists, dressmaking enthusiasts, fashion and pattern-making students, tailors and makers who want a more technical understanding of garment construction.

LEARN SEWING AND PATTERN MAKING

From sewing basics to professional garment construction

Learning sewing for beginners becomes more rewarding when each technique has a clear purpose. Gina Chiriac’s online sewing courses combine the basics of sewing for beginners with pattern making, fabric cutting and professional finishing. The two practical projects help students build skills in measuring, working with sewing patterns, handling fabric and creating precise garment details.

The complete course guides you through a made-to-measure straight skirt, from measurements and a custom pattern to fitting and finishing. The French pocket course focuses on drafting and sewing a functional pocket that sits neatly on trousers or skirts. Both provide step by step sewing for beginners as well as practical techniques for sewing enthusiasts who want to improve.

Sewing and pattern making courses: learn from body measurements

In the complete garment course you learn pattern making using real body measurements and the Chiriac Method. Build a basic skirt pattern from scratch, consider proportions and refine the fit after trying on the garment. The resulting pattern can be reused and can serve as a starting point for future skirt variations. A downloadable PDF sewing pattern with instructions and size variations is included in this course.

Understanding sewing pattern blocks and sewing with a pattern helps you see how a garment is shaped before cutting. The lessons connect pattern making for beginners with the grain line, warp, weft and bias, so you can position pieces on the fabric and cut accurately. Learning these principles can support future clothes patterns and garment projects as you develop your skills.

Machine sewing for beginners and professional finishing

The lessons demonstrate sewing with a sewing machine for beginners using domestic and industrial machines. A domestic sewing machine with a straight stitch is sufficient for the complete course. Basic familiarity can help, but no previous sewing or pattern-making experience is required. An iron and careful pressing support the construction and finishing process.

Practise sewing stitches for beginners and professional tailoring stitches as you sew darts, a back vent, an invisible zipper and a visible waistband. You will also learn to make a buttonhole and finish with a blind hem. Gina explains techniques for working without basting in the relevant stages, using precision and pressing to support the result.

French pocket: pattern, stability and sewing

The French pocket course is a focused sewing training project for anyone who wants to improve garment details. Begin with a trousers or skirt base, create the pocket pattern and decide how the opening relates to a high or low waist. Learn to choose its angle and pocket-bag depth for comfortable use.

A slanted opening can stretch because of the fabric direction. The lessons explain how reinforcement, pressing and accurate machine stitching help control distortion. You will construct the pocket bag, align the pieces and finish the pocket with care. The methods are useful foundations for working on trouser and skirt details as you continue practising.

What sewing tools and materials do you need?

For these beginner sewing projects, prepare a sewing machine, an iron, scissors, pins or clips, a measuring tape, paper and pencil for drafting, and suitable fabric. The complete garment course uses a printed copy of the supplied pattern templates; a ruler is also useful for the pocket project. The lessons introduce sewing tools and explain how they fit into each process.

If you are exploring sewing tools for beginners, start with suitable basic sewing supplies and add equipment as your work develops. Needles, thread and fabric are essential sewing supplies for machine sewing; the tools used for sewing and the sewing materials you choose should suit the fabric and project.

Who are these sewing courses for?

These online pattern making courses and sewing lessons are for beginners, self-taught makers, fashion students and people who want to build practical tailoring skills. The complete fashion sewing course brings together basic sewing classes and sewing pattern making classes in a practical project. Those comparing sewing machine courses for beginners can follow demonstrations of garment construction, while the course also introduces sewing supplies for beginners. The complete course connects sewing clothes for beginners with custom pattern drafting; the pocket course provides a concentrated study of placement, cutting and garment finishing.

If you are considering sewing and pattern making courses or pattern making and sewing classes, explore the two course pages for their full programmes. Learn sewing online at your own pace and revisit the lessons as you practise.

The demonstrations connect basic sewing lessons with darts, a back vent, an invisible zipper, a waistband and a blind hem.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about online sewing courses

Are these sewing courses suitable for beginners?

Yes. The courses explain the work progressively and are suitable for beginners who want to learn a structured professional method.

Do the courses teach pattern making as well as sewing?

Yes. Pattern drafting and garment construction are connected.

What is the Chiriac Method?

It is Gina Chiriac's structured approach to measurements, made-to-measure pattern drafting, precise cutting, sewing and professional finishing.

Can I start these sewing courses without experience?

Yes. Both courses are presented for beginners and introduce their projects step by step. Basic familiarity with a sewing machine can be helpful.

Do the courses teach sewing and pattern making?

Yes. The complete course covers measurements and drafting a skirt pattern; the French pocket course explains how to draft the pocket from a garment base.

Is a downloadable sewing pattern included?

The complete straight-skirt course includes a downloadable PDF sewing pattern with instructions and size variations.

What sewing machine do I need?

A domestic sewing machine with a straight stitch is sufficient for the complete course. The pocket course also requires a sewing machine and an iron.

What will I learn about taking measurements?

In the complete course, Gina demonstrates taking body measurements using the Chiriac Method and using them to draft a made-to-measure pattern.

Will I learn how to cut fabric?

Yes. The complete course covers grain line, warp, weft and bias, as well as positioning the pattern and cutting fabric.

Which sewing stitches and finishes are covered?

The complete course covers darts, an invisible zipper, a back vent, a waistband, a buttonhole and a blind hem.

What is the French pocket course about?

It teaches the pattern and construction of a slanted pocket, including placement, angle, pocket-bag depth, reinforcement and finishing.

Will I learn how to prevent a pocket opening from stretching?

The pocket course explains how to reinforce the slanted opening and use pressing and machine control to help prevent distortion.

Can I reuse the pattern from the complete course?

Yes. The course includes checking the fit and refining the drafted pattern so it can be reused.

What basic sewing supplies should I prepare?

Prepare a sewing machine, an iron, scissors, pins or clips and suitable fabric. A measuring tape, paper and pencil help with measurements and drafting; a ruler is useful for the pocket project.

Can I learn professional sewing techniques at home?

The online courses demonstrate professional sewing and pattern-making techniques through projects you can practise with your own equipment.

Are the courses suitable for fashion students and experienced makers?

Yes. Beginners can follow the projects step by step, while sewing enthusiasts and fashion students can explore the construction and finishing methods.

Can these skills help with other sewing projects?

Measurements, pattern drafting, cutting, sewing and finishing are useful foundations for future work. Other garments may require additional techniques.
